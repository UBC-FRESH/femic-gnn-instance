Glossary
========

This page defines the acronyms and shorthand used in the public documentation.
It is not a data dictionary for every model field.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 18 82

   * - Term
     - Meaning in this project
   * - AAC
     - Annual allowable cut: a volume target. Here it is derived and
       non-legal, not a legal harvest level.
   * - AOI
     - Area of interest: the mapped area used for an analysis.
   * - AU
     - Analysis unit: a growth-curve unit defined by TSA, stratum, and site
       index level. The model keeps TSA-prefixed AUs separate.
   * - CHM
     - Canopy height model: an estimate of vegetation height above the ground.
       It is not a field inventory.
   * - DEM
     - Digital elevation model: a gridded ground-surface elevation product.
   * - FHOPS
     - Forest Harvesting Operations Planning System: the harvest-system and
       machine-cost model used for the cost layer.
   * - GNN
     - Gwa'sala-'Nakwaxda'xw Nations.
   * - LiDAR
     - Light detection and ranging: an active remote-sensing product used for
       terrain and canopy evidence where available.
   * - Net revenue
     - Modeled log revenue minus modeled costs. It is not a realized profit.
   * - NFI
     - National Forest Inventory: a public plot source used in ground-plot
       checks.
   * - PSP
     - Permanent sample plot: a remeasured forest inventory plot. ``PSP_R`` is
       experimental and ``PSP_G`` is natural growth in this project.
   * - SI
     - Site index: a site-productivity measure, expressed here in metres.
   * - THLB
     - Timber harvesting land base: the area assumed available for timber
       harvest. In this prototype it is derived and non-legal.
   * - Transload
     - Transfer of logs between truck and barge at a terminal or landing.
   * - TSA
     - Timber supply area: the provincial management unit used to organize the
       base models. This project keeps TSA 46 and TSA 47 separate.
   * - VRI
     - Vegetation Resources Inventory: the provincial forest inventory used for
       stand attributes and polygon support.
   * - m³
     - Cubic metre, used for log volume.
   * - sph
     - Stems per hectare.

See :doc:`economic-screening`, :doc:`indicators`, and :doc:`aus` for how these
terms are used.
