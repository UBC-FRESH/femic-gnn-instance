# v0.1.0-prototype release notes

Date: 2026-10-25
Status: prototype / pre-release
Maintainer: UBC-FRESH
Boundary: proposed GNN Statement of Intent polygon
Legal status: not a legal boundary, land-base determination, or allowable cut

## What this is

A public prototype FEMIC/ws4 model for the Gwa'sala-'Nakwaxda'xw Nations (GNN)
traditional territory. It combines the TSA 46 and TSA 47 portions of the
proposed GNN Statement of Intent boundary.

This is a method and data-review release. It is not an operational plan and it
does not claim GNN confirmation.

The THLB and AAC are derived from the TSA 46/47 base models. They are not legal
determinations.

## Method

1. Clip the calibrated TSA 46/47 checkpoints to the GNN AOI.
2. Combine the clipped checkpoints; keep TSA-prefixed AU ids.
3. Combine the TSA 46/47 FEMIC bundles.
4. Export one Patchworks XML/fragments model.
5. Merge safe slivers and build/validate the ws4 matrix.
6. Build the topology and opening-first schedule.
7. Simulate and report the derived benchmark.

## Validation evidence

- Non-PSP matched sample: 34 rows / 10 sites (TSA 47 only).
- Public-plot inventory: 51 sites in the AOI.
- PSP check: 55 observations / 12 sites; 17 PSP_R experimental and 7 PSP_G
  natural sites. Median age 101; this is not a managed-young TIPSY benchmark.
- The combined evidence is still too sparse for a GNN-specific managed-curve
  accuracy conclusion.
- No curve or AAC target was tuned to the benchmark.

## Known gaps

- **Boundary.** The proposed SOI polygon is not a final treaty boundary. P1.1
  was closed as deferred by the maintainer; no external review is claimed.
- **THLB/AAC.** Derived, non-legal, and insufficient for operational, legal, or
  investment decisions. A reviewed GNN netdown is required before operational
  use.
- **TSA 46 SI/origin.** The checkpoint SI understates managed-stand SI in the
  base-model evidence. See `UBC-FRESH/femic#343`. The GNN model inherits the
  issue and does not switch curves.
- **Ground plots.** The managed/young sample is too sparse for a confident
  accuracy claim.
- **Decade 10.** The miss is a 100-year horizon and age-structure end-effect,
  not a curve or target error.
- **AUs.** TSA-prefixed AUs are retained; the reporting crosswalk is not a model
  harmonization.
- **UI.** The local UI is loopback-only. The public surface is the docs site.
