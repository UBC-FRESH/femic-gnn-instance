## Outcome

The GNN compiled dataset now has **model-wide public proxy fields** for terrain
and VRI stand attributes, plus explicit source and coverage flags. The fields
are **descriptive screening proxies only**; the LiDAR validation failed for
absolute operability use.

- Terrain: 26,762 / 26,801 fragments available; 19,331 / 19,366 block supports.
- VRI: 26,801 / 26,801 fragments and 19,366 / 19,366 block supports.
- Terrain gaps: 39 fragments/blocks, recorded as unavailable.
- LiDAR remains the 181-direct-fragment / 184-block validation pilot only.

## Public Terrain Build

- Source: AWS Open Data Terrarium terrain tiles.
- Zoom: 12; 384 cached tiles, range `x=586..609`, `y=1361..1376`.
- Source CRS: EPSG:3857; output CRS: EPSG:3005.
- Target: 4,480 x 3,004 at **30 m**, 13,457,920 valid pixels.
- Derived with Horn percent slope from the reprojected DEM.
- Cache and intermediate DEM/slope live under
  `runtime/gnn/public_terrain/`.

This is a coarse public terrain product, not LiDAR and not a survey.

## VRI Build

Used the existing public VRI-derived checkpoint:

- `LABEL_HEIGHT` -> `vri_height_m` (decimetres converted to metres)
- `CROWN_CLOSURE` -> `vri_crown_closure_pct`
- `LIVE_STAND_VOLUME_125` -> `vri_live_volume_125_m3_ha`
- `WHOLE_STEM_BIOMASS_PER_HA` -> `vri_whole_stem_biomass_ha`

VRI is aggregated to block support with area weighting and propagated to
fragments by `block_id`. It is inventory, not field truth and not LiDAR CHM.

## Validation Against the LiDAR Pilot

The comparison is 181 direct fragments / 184 blocks. Values are block-level;
public proxies are 30 m Terrarium or VRI, LiDAR is 10 m.

| Comparison | n | Proxy mean | LiDAR mean | Bias | MAE | RMSE | r |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Terrarium mean slope vs LiDAR mean slope | 184 | 19.33% | 32.99% | -13.66 | 13.97 | 16.18 | 0.63 |
| Terrarium p90 slope vs LiDAR p95 slope | 184 | 28.77% | 68.78% | -40.01 | 40.13 | 43.07 | 0.67 |
| VRI label height vs LiDAR CHM mean | 184 | 11.10 m | 10.60 m | +0.50 | 4.09 | 5.06 | -0.38 |
| VRI label height vs LiDAR CHM p95 | 184 | 11.10 m | 19.56 m | -8.46 | 8.95 | 10.70 | -0.28 |

Decision:

- Do **not** replace ground/cable/heli slope thresholds with Terrarium values.
- Do **not** treat VRI height as CHM or a block-level canopy predictor.
- Keep both field sets as descriptive/screening indicators.
- Do not extrapolate, impute, or tune a correction from the LiDAR pilot.
- The existing operability screen and volume-only release remain unchanged.

## Dataset Integration

- Dataset: `runtime/gnn/matrix`.

- `ws4 validate --dataset runtime/gnn/matrix` passes.
- `Simulator.from_dataset` loads 26,801 fragments and exposes the terrain fields.

- Core tables (`curves`, `treatments`, `tracks`, `strata`, `features`,
  `products`, `accounts`, `protoaccounts`, `groups`, `messages`) are unchanged
  versus the backup. Only `fragments`, `blocks`, and `manifest.json` changed.

## Limits

- 30 m Terrarium is coarse and smoothed; it understates steeper slopes.
- 39 block/fragment supports have no terrain value.
- VRI label height has low spatial correlation with LiDAR CHM.
- No field accuracy, DBH, true stems/ha, biomass, biodiversity, or operability
  conclusion is added.
- No curve, THLB, AAC, price, recovery, benchmark, or release label is changed.
- The GNN release remains volume-only.

## Next
