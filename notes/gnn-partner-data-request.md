**Subject:** Request for current operational, recovery, price, access, and validation data for the GNN prototype

Dear GNN project partners,

We are completing the next stage of the FEMIC/ws4 prototype for the TSA 46/47 portions of the proposed Gwa'sala-'Nakwaxda'xw Nations Statement of Intent area. The current release is a **volume-only prototype**. It is not a legal determination, a sustainable harvest level, or a financially calibrated result.

We have built a diagnostic operability, cost, and net revenue screen and a solved FHOPS marine transport model. That work has reached the limit of what public data can support. We would like your help with current operational data, or with introductions to the people who hold it.

We are **not** asking you to approve the model, change curves, or provide a treaty/title decision. We are asking for data or confirmation that will let us replace assumptions with observed or reviewed inputs.

## 1. Where the model stands

The current prototype uses:

- a proposed SOI AOI of **682,155.05 ha** inside the TSA 46/47 union;
- a derived calibrated THLB of **52,984.42 ha**;
- a derived annual AAC of **252,636.75 m3/yr**;
- 39 TSA-prefixed model AUs, with a reporting crosswalk only;
- a public ground-plot sample of **34 rows / 10 sites**, plus a PSP sample of **55 observations / 12 sites** — too sparse for a GNN-specific managed-curve accuracy conclusion;
- class-average FHOPS harvest costs;
- a provisional grade/sort recovery matrix and a local, non-redistributable BC price deck;
- a diagnostic full-THLB result of **95.5% positive** under legacy transport and **95.7% positive** under the solved Quatsino proxy. These are screening results, not a sustainable or profitable claim;
- a solved FHOPS multi-day marine proxy for **Quatsino Sound → Ladysmith Harbour**, using assumed barge capacity, rates, road legs, and terminal handling.

The largest remaining uncertainty is not the growth model. It is the **marine terminal and transport system**, followed by **harvest/access cost**, **grade/sort recovery**, and **current prices**.

## 2. What we are asking for

Please provide whatever is available, even if partial, redacted, aggregated, or limited to a single recent example. If an item cannot be shared, please tell us who can discuss it or provide an introduction.

### Priority 1 — Marine transport and terminal data

This is the current critical path. The model has a public-route proxy, but it is not a current GNN terminal, vessel, or rate deck.

| Item | What we need | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Origin terminal | Confirmed log-barge load site(s), operator, ownership, contact, and current status | The model currently uses a public candidate/proxy, not a confirmed GNN terminal |
| Destination | Confirmed sort yard, mill, export terminal, or dump; operator; acceptance requirements | Determines route, handling, and sort/grade requirements |
| Route | Actual road route from blocks/landings to the origin; actual water route, distance, and any stops | Replaces approximate waypoints and straight-line checks |
| Barge/vessel | Barge type, capacity (m3 or tonnes), load/unload time, cycle time, fleet size, towing configuration | Replaces assumed 10,000 m3 and 3,955-minute cycle |
| Rates | Current carrier rates or tariff: per trip/day/hour/tonne/m3; fuel; waiting; overtime; towing; boom tending; terminal handling; storage; minimum charges | Replaces FHOPS fixture and legacy FFCM rates |
| Utilisation | Typical load factor, seasonal variation, weather/tide constraints, availability | The unit cost is highly sensitive to utilisation |
| Destination handling | Dump/sort/boom/storage charges, throughput limits, queue/waiting, damage/reject rules | Determines terminal cost and feasibility |
| Evidence | Dated quote, contract extract, invoice, tariff sheet, or a documented verbal estimate | We need a date and provenance for every value |

If current rates are confidential, we can work with:

- an anonymised or indexed rate;
- a range with the basis stated;
- a single recent example;
- a contact for the carrier/terminal; or
- a data-sharing agreement.

### Priority 2 — Harvest, operability, access, and cost

The current screen uses class-average full-system costs. We need to know what is actually used and what it costs on the North Island.

| Item | What we need | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Harvest systems | Actual ground/cable/helicopter systems used by area, species, slope, terrain, and season | Class averages hide the non-ground share and system limits |
| Operability | Slope/terrain/geotechnical thresholds; unstable terrain; karst; riparian; cultural/archaeological exclusions; access limits | The current 35%/50% slope cutoffs are generic |
| Roads and landings | Road standard, landing spacing/size, construction and deactivation cost, haul distance, water crossings | Road/landing cost is missing from the screen |
| Machine and contractor rates | Current hourly, shift, or m3 rates; productivity; availability; move-in/move-out; overtime; fuel | Replaces FHOPS registry averages |
| Overhead and profit | Contractor overhead, supervision, insurance, mobilization, profit, or a documented margin | The current screen excludes these |
| Silviculture | Regeneration, brushing, spacing, fertilization, surveys, and other obligations | Needed for a full cost, not just harvest |
| Block data | Anonymised block/landing geometry or a representative sample; treatment, volume, age, species | Lets us test a GNN-specific block rather than an average |

### Priority 3 — Grade/sort recovery and prices

The current recovery matrix is an assumption set. The price deck is local and cannot be redistributed. Net revenue is therefore not yet a credible stand-level result.

| Item | What we need | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cruise/sort recovery | Grade/sort distribution by species, age, site, and quality class; cruise or sort-yard data | Replaces the assumption matrix |
| Mill/sort-yard recovery | Actual recovery by sort and destination; reject/waste; breakage; recovery factors | Shows what a stand actually produces |
| Sort specification | Local sawlog/peeler/utility/pulp definitions and destination requirements | Connects volume to market grade |
| Prices | Current delivered or at-market prices by species/grade, or a dated price deck; contract or netback if available | Replaces the provisional deck |
| Cypress | Second-growth cypress price/recovery, or confirmation that only an old-growth proxy is available | Cypress currently uses an old-growth proxy |
| Market limits | Any restriction on using or republishing price data | We will keep raw restricted tables internal and publish only methods/aggregates after review |

### Priority 4 — Inventory, validation, THLB, and boundary review

| Item | What we need | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Managed/young plots | PSP, CMI, or remeasured plots inside the GNN AOI, especially managed stands | The public sample is too sparse for a GNN-specific accuracy conclusion |
| TSA 46 SI/origin | The base-model owner's origin-separated SI decision; do not generalize the CWH_HW H evidence to every AU | TSA 46 is 34.7% of derived THLB; the current model uses the inherited checkpoint |
| LiDAR/terrain | Any partner-held LiDAR, DEM, or terrain/biodiversity product that should be used instead of public LidarBC or coarse DEM | The current terrain is a coarse centroid sample |
| THLB/netdown | Reviewed legal-order spatial overlay or official SELES/STSM netdown; known exclusions (reserves, private land, cultural areas, OGMAs, WHAs, karst, unstable terrain, roads) | The derived THLB is not a legal determination |
| AAC/THLB review | Confirmation of the appropriate review path for the derived values | The model currently carries derived, non-legal values |
| Boundary/public output | Confirmation of the proposed SOI boundary and which outputs may be public | The boundary review is deferred; no GNN confirmation is claimed |
| Preferred data | Any GNN-preferred boundary, tenure, or inventory dataset | We will use the authoritative source if one exists |

## 3. Minimum viable response

If a full package is not possible, the following would materially improve the model:

1. One confirmed origin terminal and one destination, with operator contacts.
2. One recent barge/vessel capacity and cycle, and one current rate or tariff.
3. Actual road and water distances for that route.
4. One current harvest-system cost or contractor rate for ground, cable, and helicopter.
5. A grade/sort recovery table or one recent cruise/sort-yard sample.
6. One dated price or netback example, including cypress if available.
7. Any managed/young plot data inside the AOI.
8. A statement of which boundary/public outputs are acceptable.

## 4. Data handling and confidentiality

We will:

- accept a data-sharing agreement or confidentiality terms;
- accept redacted, aggregated, indexed, or range data;
- keep restricted raw data in a controlled local workspace;
- not republish restricted raw tables;
- record the source, date, units, CRS, and any transformation;
- report only methods, aggregates, and derived results that you approve;
- stop and ask before using data outside the stated purpose;
- not change growth curves, THLB, AAC, prices, or recovery to force a result.

We will not treat silence or informal feedback as approval. A written response, even a short one, is the record we need.

## 5. Suggested formats

- Tables: CSV, XLSX, or delimited text with a column dictionary.
- Spatial: GeoPackage, shapefile, or GeoJSON; state the CRS (EPSG:3005 or EPSG:4326 preferred).
- Documents: PDF, DOCX, or extracted text; include the date and author.
- Rates: state currency, cost year, unit basis, inclusions/exclusions, and whether the value is a quote, contract, invoice, or estimate.
- For each item, please state: source, date, coverage, units, and whether it may be shared with UBC-FRESH and cited publicly.
